Material Arc

Expressive Design. Essential Data.

Material Arc is a free watch face for the Garmin Venu 3, published by an individual under the name Material Arc Studio. Time, weather, battery, steps, Body Battery, heart rate, and active calories sit on a quiet arc layout. It is independent work inspired by Material 3. Material Arc Studio is a public display name, not a registered company.

The active face puts a large clock at the centre, with the weekday and date above it. Four arcs follow the edge of the screen: weather, battery, steps, and Body Battery. Two gauges show heart rate and active calories. When notifications are waiting, their count appears. When the count is zero, that mark is hidden.

The weather arc places the current temperature within today's forecast low and high. Temperature is shown in Celsius. A weather observation older than three hours is not shown as current. The steps arc uses the watch's step goal. The calorie number is active calories, and the ring compares active calories with the day's total. If a reading is unavailable, the face shows a dash rather than a guess.

The always-on display keeps the time, weekday, date, and battery percentage, in a dimmer treatment of the same type. It shifts slightly as the minutes pass. A notification count appears there only when notifications are waiting.

The clock follows the watch's 12-hour or 24-hour setting. In 12-hour mode the face shows 1 through 12 and does not add an AM or PM marker. Weekday names are English. The first release supports the Venu 3. The face has no settings screen and no paid upgrade.

On the watch

  • Large clock, English weekday, and date
  • Weather, battery, steps, and Body Battery on four arcs
  • Heart rate and active calories on two gauges
  • Notification count when notifications are waiting
  • Always-on time, date, and battery
  • 12-hour and 24-hour time, following the watch
  • Garmin Venu 3
  • Free, with no settings and no in-app purchase
